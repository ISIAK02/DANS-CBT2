Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

DANS CBT helps students turn educational material into practice exams. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you create or use an account, we may collect your name or nickname, email address, profile picture, authentication details, exam answers and results, uploaded study documents, bookmarks, subscription records, and payment proof submitted for review.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with the account information you authorize, such as your name, email address, and profile picture.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure your account, provide exams and analytics, save your progress, process subscriptions and payment reviews, send verification or account emails, provide customer support, prevent abuse, and improve DANS CBT.

Service providers

DANS CBT uses trusted service providers to operate the service, including Firebase Authentication and Firestore for authentication and account data, Vercel for hosting, Cloudinary for uploaded media, and Google services when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information according to their own policies and our instructions where applicable.

Uploaded documents and payment proof

Only upload study material and payment documents that you are permitted to share. Do not upload sensitive personal information that is unrelated to using DANS CBT. Uploaded material may be processed to create practice questions and may be stored while your account is active or as needed for service, security, and legal purposes.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate DANS CBT, when required by law, to protect the service and users, or with your permission. Public leaderboard features may display the name or nickname you choose to use there, along with relevant performance information.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or security requirements. To request account or data deletion, contact us using the support channel below. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so protect your password and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Children

DANS CBT is intended for people who can lawfully use the service in their location. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact DANS CBT support through WhatsApp support.
